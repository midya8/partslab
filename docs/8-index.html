03 / OPTIMIZATION IS NOT IDENTIFICATION

Descent is not enough.

A zero can stay locked.

With identity basis and target [1,1], a plain multiplicative update initialized at [1,0] stays at [1,0]. Its loss is 0.5 and the missing coordinate has gradient -1. A coordinate-minimization sweep reaches [1,1] with zero loss.

This is a control for the plain update, not a claim about every modified multiplicative method.

The same sum, different weights.

Multiply a coefficient by 2 and divide its basis by 2: the reconstruction is unchanged. Permute parts together: still unchanged. Even a fixed dictionary can have nonunique coefficients when basis vectors duplicate.

Unit-norm display fixes one scale convention. It does not identify semantic or unique parts. A small KKT residual is not a global NMF certificate.

04 / REPRODUCE, THEN INTERPRET
Parts, not promises.
Manual cyclic coordinate minimization alternates nonnegative coefficients and a nonnegative basis. Each scalar step minimizes its bounded quadratic; every recorded loss and numerical KKT residual remains visible. The joint factorization is nonconvex. We report budget exhaustion honestly, without calling a descent curve a convergence proof.
